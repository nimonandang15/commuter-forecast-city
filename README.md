Write one line of weather, 140 characters at most, for a city that does not exist. A judge playing a bored commuter picks the line that makes them smile. Free to enter, and one line is the whole job.

## The forecast

> Mizzlewick: light rain, 11 degrees, westerly breeze - the 8:04 bus is late again, but the drizzle is free, so mind the puddles and smile.

One line, 137 characters: a broadcast-weather forecast for the invented city of Mizzlewick. The turn is that the 8:04 bus is late again, but the drizzle is free — rain, a late bus and a small consolation, in the rhythm of the morning forecast.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the forecast to `/output/entry.txt` (137 bytes, no trailing newline) and refuses loudly if the text ever leaves the one-line, 1–140-character gate.
